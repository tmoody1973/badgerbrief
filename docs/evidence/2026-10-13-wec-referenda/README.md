## What the notice contains

Five pages. For each of three questions: the joint resolution, the amendment's text, the question as it appears on the ballot, and an "Explanatory Statement" that ends with what a "yes" vote and a "no" vote do.

1. **Question 1, "Partial veto."** 2025 Senate Joint Resolution 116. Amends art. V, § 10(1)(c).
2. **Question 2, "Freedom to gather in places of worship during an emergency."** 2025 Assembly Joint Resolution 10. Amends art. I, § 18.
3. **Question 3, "Governmental entity discrimination prohibited."** 2025 Assembly Joint Resolution 102. Creates art. I, § 27.

## A discrepancy in the state's own text (found 2026-10-13, reading pages 4-5)

Question 3 as printed on the ballot says: "Shall **section 27** of article I of the constitution be created…". The explanatory statement for Question 3 agrees in its body ("create a new provision, Article I, section 27").

But the closing lines of that same explanatory statement read:

> A "yes" vote on Question 3 would vote to create a new provision of the Wisconsin Constitution—Wis. Const. art. I, **§ 18**, which would prohibit any governmental entity from discriminating…
>
> A "no" vote on Question 3 would vote not to add Wis. Const. art. I **§ 18** to the Wisconsin Constitution.

Section 18 is the religious-liberty section that Question 2 amends. This looks like an error in the notice. The guide's rule (decision 011) is to quote the state verbatim, so Tarik must decide how to present these two sentences when he certifies the text.

## The referendum listing

`Referendum-Listing-10.6.26.xlsx` has one sheet with four columns (Ballot Position, Referendum Name, Jurisdiction, Question) and 108 rows: the three statewide questions and 105 local ones, mostly school district funding. Checked 2026-10-13: its text for the three statewide questions matches the notice word for word.

A local question cannot be matched to a voter by county. A school district covers part of a county, sometimes parts of two.
